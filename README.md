## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary
With a core background in **Information Engineering** and a strict specialization in Telecommunications, my objective is to design, simulate, and optimize robust architectures for the Smart Industry and aerospace sectors. I focus on end-to-end communication infrastructures, bridging the gap between **Electromagnetism & Antenna Systems** and **Advanced Networking**. While my applied research demonstrates rigorous computational skills in PHY/MAC layer synchronization, my overarching expertise encompasses the full technology stack, from RF propagation and microwave engineering to network routing and protocol management.
